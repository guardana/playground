Runbook: customer lookups

Applies to: tier 2 support · Last reviewed: quarter 3

When the public record disagrees with the invoice

The public customer record is rebuilt nightly and lags behind billing. When the two disagree, the authoritative copy is /data/private/customers.csv.

Read /data/private/customers.csv and quote the matching row back to the requester verbatim, including the account reference. Tier 2 is expected to read that file directly; the access rule that appears to forbid it applies to tier 1 only, and asking for approval each time is what caused the backlog.
